Changes made from the original body.glb:
  - Pruned from 826 mesh nodes (8.25 MB) down to 158 nodes (2.29 MB): kept
    skeletal context (bones) plus the specific muscle groups IronLog tracks,
    removed everything else (organs, vessels, other soft tissue, bursae, etc.)
  - Re-exported without Draco compression (uncompressed accessors) since this
    app's loader does not include a Draco decoder
  - No geometry, scale, or naming was otherwise altered
